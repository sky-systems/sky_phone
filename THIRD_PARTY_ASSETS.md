# Third-party game assets

This record lives outside the deployable `sky_phone` resource and its frontend.
Sky Phone is free to download; the vehicle below must not be included in any
paid or paywalled distribution. The phone's GPL-3.0 license does not relicense
the vehicle model or textures under GPL-3.0.

## KnoWay Ocelot E-Stride 1.0

Original: [KnoWay Ocelot E-Stride](https://www.gta5-mods.com/vehicles/knoway-ocelot-e-stride).

Creator-provided attribution:

- Rockstar Games: original assets.
- Roost4R: wheel model.
- sirstirfry: KnoWay decals.
- Suzuka, also known as a_asdf556: vehicle model editing.
- JsH and HexR: original screenshots.

The creator permits project and FiveM use with attribution and prohibits
including the mod's contents in paywalled content, including Patreon. See the
original page for the published terms. Keep this attribution available when
redistributing the free project.

The four original YFT/YTD containers are unchanged. The accompanying metadata
uses isolated `KNOWAYEST_` layouts and the `NEON` electric audio profile for
Legacy build 3258 compatibility. These metadata checks do not replace FiveM
streaming, collision, seat and multiplayer acceptance tests.
